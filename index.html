[ combat ]

aim you can actually tune.

aimbot moves your camera. silent aim doesn't, the bullets just go where they should. both get their own fov, hitbox pick and priority (closest to crosshair, closest distance, lowest health).

linear mode has a curve that scales every step by how far you still are from the target: constant, linear, ease in, ease out, s-curve. the graph is drawn from the same five functions the script uses, so what you see is what you get.

about the ragebot: it comes in five styles (kh, ue, k8x, careful, fw ue). target order can put flagged hackers first, or leave them for last. your call.

[ faq ]

stuff people ask.

does it work in other games?

no. on boot it waits for rivals' controller scripts, and if they aren't there it just stops. it won't do anything weird in other games, it won't do anything at all.

where do i get a key?

in the discord. keys are handled by luarmor. paste yours into script_key above the loadstring.

how do i open the menu?

right shift. you can rebind it in settings. on mobile there's a button you can drag wherever you want it.

do i have to execute it again every server?

not if you turn on re-run after teleport in settings. it queues itself for the next server (your executor needs queue_on_teleport for that).

will i get banned?

it's a script, so there's always a chance. the ragebot is loud, legit settings are a lot quieter. don't play on an account you'd cry about.
